# Sinapeos → YouTube Repurposing Workflow

Every published article on sinapeos.com is also a YouTube video script. No extra writing required.

## Format

The 5-act structure maps directly to video:

| Article Act | Video Section | Duration |
|------------|---------------|----------|
| Act 1: Crisis at its worst | Cold open (hook) | 0:00-1:00 |
| Act 2: Wrong turns | The problem deepens | 1:00-3:00 |
| Act 3: The breakthrough | Climax/reveal | 3:00-6:00 |
| Act 4: The result | The payoff + data | 6:00-8:00 |
| Act 5: The lesson | Conclusion + CTA | 8:00-10:00 |

## Title Formula

"[PROBLEM] Was Supposed to Destroy Us. Here is What Actually Happened."

Examples:
- "The Ozone Hole Was Supposed to Destroy Us. Here is What Actually Happened."
- "Polio Was Supposed to Be Unstoppable. Here is What Actually Happened."

## Thumbnail Formula

Split-screen:
- **Left:** Crisis image (black-and-white, dramatic)
- **Right:** Result image (colorful, hopeful)
- **Text overlay:** Big bold question or contrast word

## Production Notes

- Read the article aloud — it IS the script
- Add B-roll (archival footage, photos, animations) between spoken sections
- Keep pacing tight: each act gets 1-2 minutes max
- End with: "This is the fix we didn't know we needed. See you next time."
- Include links in description back to sinapeos.com for the full article with sources
